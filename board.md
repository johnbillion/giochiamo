# Queen's Garden board layout

## Geometry of the Azul Queen's Garden board

42 hexagons in 7 rosettes of 6

The Azul Queen's Garden personal board is 42 hexagons arranged as 7 "circles of 6": 1 central rosette + 6 outer rosettes. Each rosette is 6 hexes around an empty centre. Rosettes do NOT share hexes (42 distinct).

Geometry (pointy-top hexagons, axial coordinates):
- Central rosette: empty centre at (0,0), 6 hexes at ring 1
- 6 outer rosettes: empty centres at (3,0), (3,-3), (0,-3), (-3,0), (-3,3), (0,3) — i.e. hex-distance 3 from origin in the 6 cardinal axial directions
- Each outer rosette occupies the 6 neighbours of its centre

Visible empty positions: 7 rosette centres + 6 ring-2 midpoint gaps between adjacent outer rosettes (e.g. (1,1), (-1,2), (-2,1), (-1,-1), (1,-2), (2,-1)) = 13 holes total.

Overall shape is wider than tall with flat top/bottom (because pointy-top hexes form a flat-top hex-of-hexes).
